# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and collaborators viewing the personal homepage of Arman "Pakmann" Adhitama Prawirasasra, a full-stack developer in Bandung, Indonesia. They usually arrive from a CV, LinkedIn, or a portfolio link and spend under a minute deciding whether he's worth contacting. The page must answer fast: who he is, what he can do, what he's shipped, how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio homepage (plus a printable CV page) that presents ~17 years of full-stack development across Vue, Node, Ruby/Rails, and more, alongside recent projects and skills. Success means a visitor immediately understands his range and taste, and reaches out via email, WhatsApp, or LinkedIn.

## Brand Personality

Warm, crafted, confident. The page itself is a demonstration of taste: calm editorial typography, generous air, one deliberate accent, and a playful hint of character.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS startup landing: identical icon-card grids, gradient text, glassmorphism, corporate navy/teal.
- AI-slop: an eyebrow above every section, numbered section markers everywhere, ghost cards (1px border + wide shadow), over-rounded cards, and a flat cream wash with no character.
- The previous "newspaper broadsheet" look (heavy double borders, rust accent `#8a4a2f`, Playfair everywhere) is retired.

## Design Principles

- Keep the logo: the Pakmann mark is the identity anchor and stays front and center.
- Craft is the message: the design proves the developer's taste; polish is a feature.
- Air is luxury: generous whitespace, editorial rhythm, large light serif type.
- One accent used deliberately: yellow marks the sequence and the key moments, never decoration everywhere.
- Accessible by default: contrast-safe text and reduced-motion fallbacks; content never depends on JS to appear.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Body text ≥ 4.5:1 on its background; large display text ≥ 3:1.
- Every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` fallback; reveals never gate content visibility.
- Semantic landmarks (header, nav, main, footer); focus-visible styles; keyboard-operable links.
